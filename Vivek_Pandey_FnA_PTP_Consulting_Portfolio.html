Problem-to-solution framework

A repeatable six-step transformation method.

Every engagement starts with evidence and ends with an owned action plan. Recommendations are sized to the client’s data, systems, controls and operating reality.

1

Discover & align

Clarify business goals, stakeholders, scope, pain points, constraints and success criteria.

2

Baseline & map

Review volumes, cycle times, queues, touchpoints, handoffs, controls and process variants; build SIPOC and process maps.

3

Diagnose root causes

Use Pareto, 5 Whys, fishbone, cohort / segment analysis and control-gap assessment to separate symptoms from causes.

4

Design future state

Define standard work, decision rules, exception paths, ownership, data needs, system changes and control design.

5

Prioritize & deliver

Rank initiatives by value, effort, risk and dependencies; create roadmap, backlog, business case and governance.

6

Measure & sustain

Track baseline versus target, adoption, control effectiveness and realized benefits; assign accountable owners.

Relevant experience themes

Representative transformation work.

The examples below describe solution themes and delivery experience. They are not presented as independently verified case-study results, and no client-confidential metrics are claimed.

CASE THEME 01 · WORKFLOW DIGITIZATION

From shared-mailbox handling to centralized case management

Challenge: Query handling spread across group mailboxes, limited ownership visibility and manual follow-up.

Approach: Centralized case creation, email integration, acknowledgment, routing, closure / follow-up logic, reporting and operational controls.

Deliverables: Process maps, tower / mailbox mapping, functional requirements, rules, UAT scenarios, go-live readiness and KPI definitions.

Measures to baseline: Query turnaround, backlog aging, duplicate cases, routing accuracy, reopen rate and SLA compliance.

CASE THEME 02 · AP / SUPPLIER PLATFORM

Functional solutioning for AP and supplier workflows

Challenge: Manual handoffs, fragmented supplier interactions and inconsistent visibility across process steps.

Approach: Translate business needs into workflow capabilities, clear exception paths, business rules and user-centered requirements.

Deliverables: Functional solution options, user stories, acceptance criteria, process controls, backlog and release priorities.

Measures to baseline: Manual touches, query volume, exception resolution time, first-time-right and supplier self-service adoption.

CASE THEME 03 · OPERATING MODEL

Process standardization and performance governance

Challenge: Process variations, inconsistent work instructions and limited visibility into aging and service performance.

Approach: Harmonize workflows, clarify roles and escalation paths, and define a practical performance-management cadence.

Deliverables: SIPOC, SOP framework, RACI, KPI dictionary, aging / compliance / agent-performance reporting requirements.

Measures to baseline: Process variance, avoidable rework, backlog age, SLA attainment and audit exceptions.

Confidentiality: Specific client names, screenshots, financials and actual realized benefits can be shared only where authorized. All value figures should be supported by approved baseline and post-implementation data.
