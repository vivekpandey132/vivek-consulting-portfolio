How I work

A structured path from problem to outcome

A repeatable approach built around evidence, root causes, practical design and sustained adoption.

01

Discover & align

Clarify business objectives, scope, stakeholders, constraints and success measures.

02

Baseline & map

Review volumes, cycle times, aging, handoffs and process variants; create SIPOC and process maps.

03

Diagnose root causes

Use Pareto, 5 Whys, fishbone and exception analysis to distinguish symptoms from causes.

04

Design the future state

Define standard workflows, ownership, controls, decision rules and technology needs.

05

Prioritize & deliver

Sequence initiatives by value, effort, risk and dependency; translate them into a delivery roadmap.

06

Measure & sustain

Track agreed KPIs, adoption, control effectiveness and benefits against an approved baseline.

Relevant experience

Transformation themes grounded in delivery experience

Representative examples of the kinds of problems, functional design and deliverables I have worked with. Client-specific results are not implied.

Workflow digitization

From shared-mailbox handling to centralized case management

Challenge: Query handling spread across group mailboxes with manual follow-up and limited ownership visibility.

Approach: Centralized case creation, email integration, acknowledgements, routing, follow-up logic and operational reporting.

Deliverables: Process maps, functional requirements, rules, UAT scenarios and KPI definitions.

AP / supplier workflows

Functional solutioning for AP and supplier processes

Challenge: Manual handoffs and inconsistent visibility across process steps.

Approach: Define workflow capabilities, exception paths, business rules and user-centered requirements.

Deliverables: Solution options, user stories, acceptance criteria, controls and release priorities.

Process excellence

Standardization and performance governance

Challenge: Process variation and limited visibility into aging and service performance.

Approach: Harmonize workflows, clarify ownership and establish practical KPI governance.

Deliverables: SIPOC, SOP framework, RACI, KPI dictionary and reporting requirements.

Practical perspective

Transformation starts with the process—not the tool

Automation magnifies the process it is built on. Before investing in AI or workflow technology, identify the source of exceptions, standardize decision rules, improve data quality and agree how value will be measured.

  • Separate policy gaps from process and system gaps.
  • Fix recurring upstream causes before automating workarounds.
  • Build human review and auditability into AI-enabled workflows.
  • Assign a business owner to every KPI and benefit.
