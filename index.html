About Me

A transformation partner who connects process, product and delivery

I’m Vivek Pandey, an F&A and digital transformation professional with 14+ years of experience across Finance & Accounting, Procure-to-Pay (PTP), ERP-enabled operations, product management and transformation delivery. I work at the intersection of business needs, process design, technology enablement and execution—helping organizations turn complex operational challenges into structured, implementable improvement plans.

My professional perspective

I believe effective transformation begins with understanding how work actually gets done—not with selecting a tool or introducing automation in isolation. Sustainable results come from addressing root causes, simplifying process variants, clarifying ownership, strengthening controls and then applying technology where it creates practical value.

My approach combines a consultant’s structured problem-solving, a product manager’s focus on user and business outcomes, and a delivery leader’s attention to execution, governance and adoption. That means balancing strategic direction with the detail required to make a future-state process work in day-to-day operations.

Selected experience & achievements

  • Process study and current-state assessment: Review end-to-end workflows, stakeholder handoffs, process variants, work queues, policy steps and system touchpoints to understand how work is actually performed and where effort or delays accumulate.
  • Gap identification and root-cause analysis: Compare current practices with business requirements, controls and target-state expectations; use process mapping, SIPOC, Pareto analysis and 5 Whys to distinguish root causes from symptoms and document actionable gaps.
  • Process improvement recommendations: Identify opportunities to simplify steps, remove non-value-added activity, standardize work instructions, clarify ownership, improve upstream inputs and reduce avoidable rework—prioritized by business value, effort and risk.
  • Automation opportunity assessment: Identify repetitive, rules-based and high-volume activities that may be suitable for workflow automation, OCR / intelligent document processing, system integration or AI assistance. Validate feasibility, data readiness, controls and human-review requirements before recommending a solution.
  • Cycle-time and productivity improvement: Examine queue aging, approval delays, handoffs, exception volumes and manual touches; define practical interventions and baseline measures to help teams target shorter turnaround times and improved throughput without claiming unverified results.
  • PTP / AP functional solutioning: Translate invoice-processing and supplier-query needs into workflows, business rules, exception paths, user stories, acceptance criteria and delivery priorities.
  • Case management transformation: Contributed to the design and delivery of a centralized Case Management System to move query handling away from manual, shared-mailbox-based ways of working towards structured case creation, routing, acknowledgements, follow-up handling and reporting.
  • Product and release delivery: Worked across backlog refinement, sprint planning, stakeholder alignment, UAT and release readiness to help teams deliver practical business capabilities.
  • Operational governance: Focused on visibility into aging, service performance, ownership, exception handling and KPI reporting to support better day-to-day decisions.
  • Program and people leadership: Experience includes leading cross-functional teams and working within programs/projects with reported exposure to budgets and delivery scope of $20M+; scope and attribution should be confirmed for any external use.
Integrity of claims: These are experience themes and delivery contributions, not quantified client outcomes. Financial savings, cycle-time reductions or percentage improvements should only be published when supported by approved baseline and post-implementation evidence.

Practical perspective

Transformation starts with the process—not the tool

Automation magnifies the process it is built on. Before investing in AI or workflow technology, identify the source of exceptions, standardize decision rules, improve data quality and agree how value will be measured.

  • Separate policy gaps from process and system gaps.
  • Fix recurring upstream causes before automating workarounds.
  • Build human review and auditability into AI-enabled workflows.
  • Assign a business owner to every KPI and benefit.
